## Overview

Searches news, research, and social platforms for the past week's most relevant content at the intersection of AI and sustainability. Covers five themes: environmental and social impact of AI, AI for sustainable outcomes, efficient AI use, workflow productivity for consultants and educators, and general AI headlines.

**Suggested schedule:** Every Wednesday at 6:30 PM

**Designed for:** Sustainability consultants, educators, and professionals who want a weekly digest without manual searching.

## What it produces

- Structured Markdown summary with 5 themed sections
- Social dialogue highlights from Reddit and LinkedIn
- Full list of search queries used (for transparency and reproducibility)
- Saved `.md` file named with today's date

## How to use

Paste the skill prompt into Claude with the instruction: *"Create a scheduled task with this prompt, running every Wednesday at 6:30 PM"* — or run it manually any time.
